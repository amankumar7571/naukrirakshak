Fake job offers are stealing ₹5,000–₹50,000 from Indian freshers every day. A WhatsApp message from "HR", an offer letter with no interview, a "refundable security deposit" — and the money is gone.

I built NaukriRakshak to fight that. Paste any job message, posting or offer letter, and it tells you in seconds whether it's a scam — and shows you exactly why.

What makes it different: it's a real trained model, not a chatbot wrapper. TF-IDF + logistic regression, trained on 117 labelled examples of real scam vs genuine fresher postings — 24/24 on held-out tests. The whole model runs in your browser: no server, no API key, nothing you paste ever leaves your phone. And every verdict comes with its evidence and an honest "uncertain" band for the grey cases, because a wrong "genuine" is worse than no answer at all.
